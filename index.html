“I don't perform worship. I open a door and stand out of the way.”

Dr AjokeSings writes and sings from the meeting point of scripture, memory, and modern African sound — songs built for the room to disappear and the presence to remain. Fourteen years into ministry, across twenty-three nations, the work has stayed the same: make space, then get out of the way.
