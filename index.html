Symphony of Praise and Worship

(SPAW) Talent Quest with Dr AjokeSings

Come on board. Applications are open.

Calling vocalists, songwriters, instrumentalists and producers: this is your invitation to take part. The Talent Quest is how new voices reach the SPAW stage. Apply with your own material; the Quest itself is held live at Regal Hall, Daystar Christian Center, the day before the SPAW Global Concert, and every applicant hears back, whichever way the answer goes.

  • Funded studio timeStudio days toward one original recording.
  • The SPAW stageA performance slot at the SPAW Global Concert.
  • Released properlyMixed, mastered and released on the streaming platforms under your own name.
  • Photography and artworkA photo and video session, plus cover artwork.

“I don't perform worship. I open a door and stand out of the way.”

Dr AjokeSings writes and sings from the meeting point of scripture, memory, and modern African sound — songs built for the room to disappear and the presence to remain. Fourteen years into ministry, across twenty-three nations, the work has stayed the same: make space, then get out of the way.
